The trilingual inscription of Šābuhr I
at the Kaʿba-ye Zartošt

ŠKZ · aligned corpus, named entities, places

The Greek, Parthian and Middle Persian versions of the inscription, aligned at both sentence and word level, with a dataset of the named entities and a gazetteer of every place the text names.

Line numbering follows Huyse (1999). The Greek text is taken from the digital epigraphy collection of the Packard Humanities Institute, which uses the edition of Canali De Rossi (2004); the Parthian and Middle Persian versions follow Huyse's edition, the Parthian by way of Jake Nabel's Parthian Sources and the Middle Persian added here. The alignments were produced with the Ugarit alignment tool and are also browsable on Ugarit.

The datasets

Sentence-level corpus 51 ŠKZ lines
The three versions side by side, aligned segment by segment on Huyse’s line numbering, with the Parthian in both transliteration and transcription.
TSVCSVXLSX
Word-level alignment pairs 1,013 pairs
Greek, Parthian and Middle Persian correspondences with their frequency in the line, as one table or as one file per line.
TSVCSVXLSXper line (ZIP)
Named entities 412 attestations
Classified by hand as persons (PER), locations (LOC) or location derivatives (LOCderiv), across all three versions, with the line number and, for many, a Wikidata item.
TSVCSV
Place gazetteer 149 places, 134 located
One row per place behind the location attestations: identification and certainty, Pleiades and Wikidata identifiers, coordinates, and the Glossar entry and book page each identification rests on. Column-by-column documentation.
TSVmap

Huyse 1999 is in copyright, and these files point to it rather than reproduce it. Each place record carries his lemma, his ON/LN/VN type and the book page, so every identification can be checked against the printed page.
